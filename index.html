Comprehensive medical information on our treatments, allergy diagnostics, and clinical appointments.

Dr. Ajay Mohan holds M.B.B.S. and M.D. (Skin & V.D.) degrees as a recognized Gold Medalist. He brings over 10+ years of high-volume clinical excellence having served at prestigious institutions including LLRM Medical College Meerut (U.P.) and PGI (Mini). He is a celebrated cosmetologist, trichologist, and laser surgeon trusted across Purvanchal and India.
At AMS Skin & Allergy Center®, we identify the root immune trigger rather than just masking symptoms with temporary antihistamines. Through detailed clinical history, dermascopy, and allergen identification, Dr. Ajay Mohan prescribes targeted desensitization therapy, barrier-repair regimens, and preventive precautions that offer permanent relief from recurrent itching and urticaria.
Our clinic utilizes US-FDA approved medical lasers tailored specifically for Indian skin types (Fitzpatrick types III-V). The concentrated light selectively destroys hair follicles in the active growth phase without damaging surrounding dermal tissue. It is virtually painless, completely safe, and provides permanent hair reduction in 6 to 8 sessions.
Depending on scar depth (rolling, boxcar, or icepick scars), Dr. Ajay Mohan crafts multi-modality treatment plans utilizing chemical peels, microdermabrasion, subcision, and laser skin resurfacing. This combination releases tethered fibrotic bands and stimulates natural collagen remodeling for smoother, renewed skin.
Unlike salon facials, our clinical Hydrafacial uses patented vortex-suction technology to clear clogged sebum, extract stubborn comedones, and infuse high-potency antioxidants and hyaluronic acid. It delivers immediate radiance, deeply clarifies skin pores, and requires zero recovery time.
Yes. Many patients present with chronic steroid-modified fungal infections caused by over-the-counter mixed creams. Dr. Ajay Mohan implements evidence-based systemic antifungal protocols, topical barrier therapy, and strict hygiene protocols that completely clear stubborn dermatophytosis without relapse.
We conduct computerized dermascopic evaluations to assess whether patches are active or stable. Treatment includes immunomodulatory medications, topical repigmenting agents, medical phototherapy, and in stable non-responsive cases, surgical melanocyte grafting procedures.
We evaluate scalp root viability using Video Dermascopy. For early-stage thinning and telogen effluvium, we provide specialized pharmacological and growth factor therapies. For advanced androgenetic alopecia, our center performs advanced follicular Hair Transplant surgery for natural, lifelong hair density.
Radio Cautery utilizes low-temperature radiofrequency energy to gently vaporize benign skin lesions, warts, and tags under local anesthesia. The procedure takes only a few minutes, involves zero bleeding, does not require stitches, and heals without conspicuous scarring.
